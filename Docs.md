In a pet clinic or veterinary hospital, visit types are typically categorized by the reason for the appointment, the
urgency, and whether a veterinarian or a veterinary technician handles it.

Here is a breakdown of the standard visit types:

1. Routine & Preventative Care
   Puppy / Kitten Exam: Initial health check, core vaccinations, deworming, microchipping, and lifestyle guidance.
   Annual Wellness Exam: Yearly comprehensive physical checkup for healthy adult pets, including routine vaccines,
   bloodwork, and parasite screening.
   Senior Pet Wellness Exam: Bi-annual or annual comprehensive exams for aging pets (often includes arthritis checks,
   organ function panels, and mobility assessments).
   Health Certificate / Travel Exam: Documentation and physical exam required for domestic or international travel.
2. Illness & Problem-Specific Visits
   Sick Pet / Illness Exam: For pets showing signs of disease (e.g., vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, coughing, loss of
   appetite).
   Dermatology / Ear Exam: Specifically for itchy skin, hair loss, allergies, hotspots, or ear infections.
   Lameness / Orthopedic Exam: For limping, difficulty walking, joint stiffness, or suspected injury.
   Eye / Ophthalmic Exam: For redness, discharge, cloudiness, squinting, or vision loss.
3. Urgent & Emergency Visits
   Urgent Care (Same-Day): For issues that need attention within hours but are not immediately life-threatening (e.g.,
   minor bite wounds, urinary tract infections, sudden severe vomiting).
   Emergency / Critical Care: Immediate, life-threatening situations requiring triage (e.g., hit by a car, poison
   ingestion, breathing difficulty, GDV/bloat, seizures).
4. Surgical & Dental Procedures (Drop-Off Visits)
   Elective Surgery: Spaying, neutering, or declawing.
   Non-Elective / Soft Tissue Surgery: Mass/tumor removal, laceration repair, or foreign object removal.
   Dental Cleaning / Procedure (COHAT): Dental scaling, polishing, X-rays, and tooth extractions under general
   anesthesia.
5. Follow-Up & Monitoring
   Recheck / Progress Exam: To monitor a previously diagnosed illness, wound healing, or response to medication (e.g.,
   checking an ear infection after 2 weeks).
   Chronic Disease Monitoring: Regular bloodwork and exams for ongoing conditions like diabetes, kidney failure, thyroid
   disease, or epilepsy.
6. Technician / Nurse Visits (Non-Doctor Appointments)
   Vaccine Booster Visits: For pets that only need a scheduled booster and no physical exam by the doctor.
   Suture / Staple Removal: Post-surgery stitch removal.
   Maintenance / Tech Care: Nail trims, anal gland expressions, microchip implantation, or ear flushes.
   Lab Work / Sample Collection: Drop-in blood draws, fecal sample drop-offs, or urine collection.
7. Consultations & Special Visits
   Behavior Consultation: Addressing anxiety, aggression, or litter box issues.
   Nutritional / Weight Management Consultation: Custom diet plans for obesity or health-related dietary needs.
   Second Opinion / Referral Consultation: In-depth review of another clinic's medical records.
8. End-of-Life Care
   Quality of Life (QoL) Consultation: Assessment with the veterinarian to evaluate the pet's comfort and discuss
   hospice care.

Humane Euthanasia: Compassionate, peaceful end-of-life appointment.
